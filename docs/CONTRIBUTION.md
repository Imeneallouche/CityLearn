# Contribution Guidelines
- We will follow a mono repo, just to centralize our codebase, each package: [RL](../rl/) and [5G](../5g/) can be thought of as a standalone repo within your work. Please take ownership.
- Let's please always submit PRs and keep `main` branch clean. Branch standard is `<package>/<name>/<feature>`, example: `rl/kamel/train-agent`.
- For technical documentation, RL team to document in [RL Docs](./RL.md) and 5G in [5G Docs](./5G.md)
